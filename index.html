About

I am a final-year BSc Computer Science student at the University of Nottingham, studying through the University of Nottingham Ningbo China–University of Nottingham 2+2 pathway (expected July 2027). My research focuses on reliable language-model decision-making, including answer-or-abstain monitoring, evaluation validity, uncertainty and evidence signals, and the cost of additional model computation.

I am interested in extending rigorous evaluation and decision-theoretic ideas toward reliable foundation-model agents, especially evidence-grounded decisions and adaptive intervention.

Research

Context-disjoint evaluation of answer-or-abstain routing

ADMA 2026 RDI Special Session · Accepted short paper

Investigated context reuse and component exposure in an LLM answer-or-abstain pipeline and rebuilt its evaluation using context-disjoint partitions, strict model/threshold selection and a frozen test set.

Generation-derived abstention signals: incremental value and cost

IEEE Big Data 2026 IDM · Manuscript submitted, decision pending

Compared direct decisions, low-cost monitoring features, and richer generation-derived signals across two language-model backbones; examined external transfer, cost, and the robustness of claimed incremental gains.

Calibration budgets and risk certification

ACM SAC 2027 · Manuscript submitted, decision pending

Studied how the allocation of calibration data across document breadth and question depth interacts with statistical risk certifiers under matched acquisition budgets.
